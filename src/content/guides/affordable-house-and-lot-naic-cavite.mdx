## Why buyers choose Naic

- **Lower prices.** House and lot units in Naic generally cost less than similar homes in Bacoor, Imus, or Dasmariñas.
- **New developments.** Several developers have launched subdivisions in Naic, including affordable townhouses and single-attached homes that qualify for Pag-IBIG financing.
- **Room to grow.** Bigger lot cuts are easier to find than in the more crowded northern Cavite cities.
- **Coastal-town living**, with a laid-back environment that many families prefer for raising kids.

## What to check before you buy

1. **License to Sell.** Ask for the project's DHSUD License to Sell, especially for pre-selling units.
2. **Flood history.** Ask about the area's flooding history, and visit after heavy rain if you can.
3. **Water and electricity.** Confirm the water provider and when electricity will be connected.
4. **Access road.** Check how far the subdivision is from the main highway and whether there's public transport.
5. **Total cost.** Ask for the full computation: reservation fee, equity, miscellaneous fees, and monthly amortization.
